# DEMO / SYNTHETIC DATA — retail returns and exchanges

**Fictional sample policy. Not a real retailer policy, consumer-rights statement, or legal rule.**

## Return window and condition

The fictional Rang Bazaar stores in Jaipur, Chandigarh, and Ludhiana accept a return request within 7 calendar days for an unused item in its original packaging with the receipt or order ID. Staff inspect the item and check the purchase record. This sample window is invented and is not a statement of legal rights.

## Refunds and exchanges

For an approved return, the sample store initiates a refund to the original payment method; a card or UPI provider may take additional time to post it. An exchange may be offered if the requested size or item is available. A different item can have a different price, so confirm any balance in ₹ before completing the exchange. Do not promise cash for a digital payment without a manager's approval.

## Damaged or incorrect product

If the item arrived damaged or differs from the receipt, apologize and ask for the order ID plus a photo that does not expose personal documents. Escalate a repeated quality issue or a high-value purchase to the branch manager. If a GST invoice is needed, ask for the transaction ID and the billing details required by the fictional store; never request unrelated identity information.
