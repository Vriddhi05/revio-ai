# DEMO / SYNTHETIC DATA — customer service escalation guide

**Fictional sample policy. Not an actual company process or customer-service guarantee.**

## First response

The fictional REVIO demo businesses acknowledge a complaint with a brief, respectful message and confirm what outcome the customer is asking for. Do not blame a named staff member or make a refund, replacement, or deadline promise before checking the record. Capture the branch, visit or order date, and a safe contact method in the workspace.

## Escalation levels

Send payment mismatch, repeated order failure, suspected food safety concern, injury, or a complaint involving personal safety to the on-duty manager immediately. Route repeated unresolved cases to the fictional customer care lead. Mark a case urgent when the customer describes an immediate safety risk; a manager must respond directly.

## Updates and privacy

For a standard unresolved case, the sample team gives an update within one business day even if the investigation is ongoing. Keep updates factual and record the next owner. Never request an OTP, UPI PIN, full card number, Aadhaar number, or account password in a review reply or support note. Move sensitive details to an approved private channel and redact them from shared notes.
