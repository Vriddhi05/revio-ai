# DEMO / SYNTHETIC DATA — takeaway and delivery guide

**Fictional sample policy. Not a real Zomato, Swiggy, restaurant, or delivery-platform rule.**

## Late takeaway order

For takeaway orders placed with the fictional Saffron Courtyard, staff check the kitchen ticket and the hand-off time before estimating arrival. If an order is more than 20 minutes past the estimate, the branch contacts the customer with the current status and offers the available choices: continue waiting, collect the order, or cancel for a review of the eligible refund. Do not mark an order delivered until hand-off is confirmed.

## Orders placed through a delivery app

For an order placed through a marketplace such as Zomato or Swiggy, the sample restaurant records the order ID and directs payment, rider, and in-app refund questions to that marketplace's help flow. Restaurant staff can still check food preparation and packaging status. Do not claim the marketplace has approved a refund unless the status is visible in the order record.

## Missing or incorrect items

Ask which item is missing or incorrect and compare it with the kitchen ticket. If the restaurant packed the wrong item, the branch manager may offer a replacement or a partial refund after checking availability and the order terms. Keep the original packaging until the case is reviewed when practical; never ask the customer to travel back during unsafe weather just to prove an issue.
