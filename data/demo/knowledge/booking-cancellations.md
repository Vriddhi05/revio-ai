# DEMO / SYNTHETIC DATA — booking and cancellation guide

**Fictional sample policy. Not a real hotel, restaurant, or legal cancellation rule.**

## Restaurant reservations

For a table at the fictional Saffron Courtyard in Jaipur, Chandigarh, or Ludhiana, a guest may change the party size or arrival time by contacting the branch. During Friday and Saturday peak hours, the sample restaurant holds a confirmed table for 15 minutes after the booked time. Staff should call once before releasing a late reservation and note the guest's preference when possible.

## Hotel booking cancellation

The fictional Cedar Courtyard Hotel accepts a cancellation without a sample service fee until 48 hours before check-in. A cancellation inside 48 hours may be charged one night's sample room rate; a no-show may be charged one night. These are invented demo terms, and staff must check the booking confirmation before quoting them. Refunds for prepaid bookings are initiated to the original payment method after the property confirms cancellation.

## Changes and escalation

Guests should share the booking ID and the name on the reservation, but must not send card PINs or account passwords. If a guest disputes a fee or cannot travel due to an urgent situation, route the case to the duty manager for a human review rather than promising an exception. The sample desk aims to reply by the next business day.
