# DEMO / SYNTHETIC DATA — restaurant service recovery guide

**Fictional sample policy. Not a real restaurant, food-safety, or legal policy.**

## Wait times and peak-hour crowding

The fictional Saffron Courtyard dining rooms can be crowded during weekend dinner and festival periods. If a confirmed table is delayed by more than 20 minutes, the host gives an honest wait estimate and asks whether the guests prefer to wait or cancel the reservation. Never describe an unconfirmed table as ready. A manager may offer a non-cash courtesy item after reviewing the delay and availability.

## Food or service complaint

Listen without interrupting, repeat the issue to confirm it, and notify the floor manager. For a cold, missing, or incorrectly prepared dish, the manager checks the order and offers a suitable replacement when the kitchen can prepare it. For an allergy or possible food-safety concern, stop service of the affected dish and alert the manager immediately; do not speculate about medical causes in a public reply.

## Payment, invoice, and recovery

For a UPI dispute, record the amount in ₹ and masked transaction reference and follow the separate UPI refund guide. A customer who requests a GST invoice should be connected to the billing desk with the receipt details. Staff must not request an OTP, UPI PIN, or full bank credentials. Record the resolution and the person responsible for any follow-up.
