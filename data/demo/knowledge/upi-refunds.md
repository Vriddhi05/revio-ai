# DEMO / SYNTHETIC DATA — UPI refund guide

**Fictional sample policy. Not a real business, legal rule, or payment-provider promise.**

## UPI payment marked successful but order not confirmed

For the fictional Saffron Courtyard restaurants in Jaipur, Chandigarh, and Ludhiana, staff first check the order ledger against the UPI transaction reference. Never ask a customer to share a UPI PIN, OTP, password, or full bank credentials. A screenshot may be requested only after the customer hides account details.

If the payment is captured but the order is missing, record the amount in ₹, transaction time, and UTR/reference number in the private support case. The sample desk checks the payment reconciliation queue within one business day. Do not ask the customer to pay a second time until the first transaction has been checked.

## Failed or duplicated UPI payment

When PhonePe or Google Pay shows a failed payment but the bank has debited the amount, tell the customer that the sample team will check the UTR and payment status. The fictional guide allows up to 7 business days for a bank-side reversal; this is an invented service estimate, not a guarantee. If two debits appear for one order, escalate both references to the payments desk and keep the case open until one debit is confirmed reversed.

## Refund destination and updates

For a confirmed duplicate debit or an order cancelled by the fictional restaurant, the sample process sends the refund to the original payment method. Ask for a masked UTR/reference, never a PIN or OTP. Send an update after the reconciliation check and again if the expected 7-business-day window passes. A cash refund must not be promised for a UPI transaction.
